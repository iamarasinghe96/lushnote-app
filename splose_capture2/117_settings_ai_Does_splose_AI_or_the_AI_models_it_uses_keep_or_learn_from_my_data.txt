Does splose AI, or the AI models it uses, keep or learn from my data?

No. splose AI uses your information to create your response, and the AI models don't keep it afterwards or learn from it. Your notes and the content splose AI generates are saved within splose's own systems in your region, with the same protections as the rest of your data.
