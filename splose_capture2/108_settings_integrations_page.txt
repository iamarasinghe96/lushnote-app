HICAPS
HICAPS is an online claiming platform that allows you to easily claim invoices to the TAC, Worksafe Victoria, NDIS, Medibank, NIB and more. With HICAPS and splose, you can run a pre-determination for NDIS plan managed invoices and submit them for fast payment.

Tyro Health
Tyro Health enables healthcare providers to process digital payments and claims online. This includes Medicare (Bulk Bill and Patient Claims), DVA, Health fund claims and contactless debit and credit cards (via SMS, link or entering a card directly within an invoice in splose).

Zoom
